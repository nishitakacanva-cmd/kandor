About Kandor
Our mission, our data, and why we built this.
kandor
Version 1.0 · Reveal the thread
Our mission

Kandor exists because the invisible connections between products, people, and planet are being deliberately hidden. Every pack of noodles has a story — of sourcing, of labour, of chemistry, of consequence.

We believe consumers deserve to see that thread. Not as guilt, but as information. To make better choices, freely.

Data sources

FSSAI — Food Safety and Standards Authority of India

Open Food Facts — Global open-source food database

EWG Food Scores — Environmental Working Group

Brand disclosures + community contributions

Independence guarantee

No brand pays us. No advertiser influences our scores. If you ever spot a conflict of interest, flag it.
